Our Services

We construct progressive action plans for the future, based on each community's unique strengths, values, and needs. Each engagement is a unique opportunity to empower and energize students and families, aiming for educational success through culturally responsive and equity-focused approaches.

Policy & System Navigation

Support students and families in understanding BC's education system (K–12, post-secondary, adult learning). Advise school districts, colleges, and universities on Indigenous rights (UNDRIP, TRC Calls to Action, DRIPA) and equity policies for immigrants. Conduct policy analysis to identify systemic barriers and recommend reforms.

  • Education System Guidance
  • Policy Analysis & Advocacy
  • Rights-Based Consulting
  • Systemic Barrier Identification

Research & Program Development

Conduct needs assessments to identify educational gaps within Indigenous and immigrant communities. Design and evaluate pilot programs (e.g., after-school tutoring, scholarship access, leadership training). Partner with universities and community organizations to produce community-led research on equity in education.

  • Needs Assessments
  • Program Design & Evaluation
  • Community-Led Research
  • Partnership Development

Monitoring & Evaluation

Develop comprehensive monitoring frameworks to track program effectiveness and educational outcomes. Provide data-driven insights to improve services and support evidence-based decision making for educational programs serving Indigenous and immigrant communities.

About ReclaimEdu

ReclaimEdu is dedicated to reconciliation through education and empowerment through learning. We combine community's unique assets with culturally responsive educational consulting. We form deep personal connections on a personalized level as trustworthy and reliable advocates for each individual, family, and community we serve.

Our mission is to break down systemic barriers in education and create pathways for success for Indigenous communities, immigrant families, and all equity-seeking groups. We believe in the power of education to transform lives and communities.
