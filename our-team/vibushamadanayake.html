Vibusha Madanayake

Vibusha Madanayake (she/her) is a public health advocate, intersectional feminist educator, and co- founder of Reclaim.Edu. With over a decade of experience in community education, grassroots research, and feminist policy work across Sri Lanka and Canada, her work uplifts the voices and futures of women, youth, and 2SLGBTIQA+ communities. Currently based on the unceded ancestral territories of the Lheidli T’enneh (Prince George, BC), Vibusha leads culturally safe education and harm-reduction programming at Positive Living North, an Indigenous- led HIV/AIDS service organization. She’s passionate about creating learning spaces that are decolonial, trauma-informed, and rooted in care. Previously, she worked with international organizations such as UNDP, USAID, and the Open Society Foundations to support war-affected communities in Sri Lanka. She co-founded several social enterprises, including DocuWiz Pvt. Ltd. and Naari Politics—a feminist digital storytelling platform. Vibusha holds two Master’s degrees in International Relations and Gender Studies, and completed a fellowship in Public Health Advocacy at Johns Hopkins University. Her lived and professional experiences continue to shape her vision for inclusive, community-driven education that reclaims possibility.
